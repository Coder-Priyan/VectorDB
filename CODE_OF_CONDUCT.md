# VectorDB Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As contributors and maintainers of VectorDB, we are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal beliefs.

## Expected Standards

We encourage behaviors that foster an open and collaborative learning environment:
- Demonstrating empathy, kindness, and patience toward fellow community members.
- Offering constructive, actionable feedback and graciously accepting critique.
- Focusing on technical excellence, transparency, and respectful collaboration.
- Valuing diverse perspectives and approaches to learning vector databases and search.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behaviors include harassment, abusive language, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, and any conduct that disrupts a productive and respectful open-source community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these community standards and will take fair and appropriate corrective action in response to any unacceptable behavior.
